The pitch

A home for work that deserves more than a scroll

Feed platforms pay you in attention you don't own. ArtiStanza pays you in members you do.

1

You set the price

Publish free to build reach, hold your best work for a paid tier, or sell a single piece outright. Any mix, per post, changed whenever you want.

2

They come to you

Membership lives on your page, not inside an algorithm. Your readers get a feed of you, not a platform. Churn is a conversation, not a mystery.

3

One home, many crafts

Poems now. Recorded readings, galleries, and downloads later. Same page, same membership, same members. You are not locked into one format.

The difference in one line: other platforms give you an audience you rent. ArtiStanza gives you a membership you own — the reader list, the email addresses, and the archive all stay yours.

The problem

Where creators actually get stuck

What happens today

  • A poem competes with a video, a meme, and an ad in the same feed.
  • "Boost this post" becomes the only growth lever, so it quietly becomes the strategy.
  • Fan communities live in a platform's DMs, not in anything you control.
  • Paid membership is a separate product bolted onto a different site.
  • Switching medium means starting over. The tooling is single-format.
  • The algorithm is the editor. It changes and your reach changes with it.

What ArtiStanza does instead

  • Your work is the whole page. Nothing to compete against.
  • Growth comes from members telling other members. Slow, then durable.
  • The member list, emails, and archive are yours, exportable, always.
  • Membership is native: tiering and paywalls are part of publishing.
  • Poems, audio, and galleries share one model. Add a format, keep the page.
  • You choose what is public and what is for members. The platform enforces it.

How it works

Publish, gate, get paid

Three actions. The paywall is a setting on the post, not a separate system.

1

Write and publish

Drop in a poem, a story, a recording, a gallery. Save as draft, schedule it, or post it now. Drafts stay private until you say otherwise.

2

Choose who sees it

Free for everyone, free for members only, locked to a tier you name, or a one-off price. For long work, lock chapters and leave the first free as the hook.

3

Keep the money

Monthly and annual tiers, tips, and individual sales. Regular payouts, a clear fee breakdown, no lock-in.

Formats

Poetry first. Not poetry only.

We start as a serious literary platform because that's where the need is sharpest. But we refused to build a poetry-shaped box and hope it fits later. A poem, a recording, and a gallery are stored the same way, so adding one is a switch rather than a rebuild.

Why this isn't a promise we might break: we tested it. We built a poetry and prose site, then attached a brand-new format that had never been anticipated — without altering a single existing post or rebuilding the system underneath. That is the difference between a plan and a fact.

Ownership

Built so your archive outlives us

You are trusting us with the only copy of something you spent months on. That is a reasonable thing to be cautious about, so the answer is structural rather than a promise in a policy document: we are built so that losing us is survivable.

  • Export your full member list with emails and amounts, any time, in one click.
  • Export every post you have written, with its details, in open formats.
  • Your work is stored as ordinary files — plain text, standard images, standard audio.
  • No ads on your work. No selling your reader list to advertisers.
  • No sudden rule changes. If a fee changes, existing members are grandfathered.

Poetry is not a special case

We did not build a poetry app and bolt audio on afterwards. We built one home for work, and a poem simply happens to be the first thing to move in.

  • Every piece is stored whole — the poem, the recording, the gallery — in one uniform way, so nothing gets second-class treatment later.
  • Long work splits into chapters or stanzas, and each one carries its own lock. Lock the first, free the rest.
  • We proved it rather than claiming it: a completely new format was added to the live system without touching or republishing a single existing piece.
